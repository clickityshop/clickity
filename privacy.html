Privacy policy

When you place an order, Clickity collects the name, email, preferred pickup time and place, optional note, and items you submit. The Clickity owner uses this information to prepare your order, arrange pickup, and contact you about it.

Order details are stored in Clickity's order service and are visible to the shop owner through the private order dashboard. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising. This shop doesn't take online payments.

Please don't share someone else's order details. Use the shop respectfully and don't try to break it. You can absolutely tell your friends about Clickity; if anything here makes you uncomfortable, tell a trusted adult.

If you have a question about information you submitted, email clickityshop.com@gmail.com.
